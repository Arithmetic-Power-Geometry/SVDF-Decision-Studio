Mohammad Amir Khusru Akhtar · Usha Martin University, Ranchi

Start with the problem, not the score

Many organisations have several worthwhile projects but limited money, time, people and managerial attention. SVDF asks which alternatives cannot be clearly beaten on both shared value and implementation complexity.

Decision support, not automatic approval. Budget, engineering, law, safety, ethics and local priorities remain separate decisions.
