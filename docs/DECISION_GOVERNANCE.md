# Decision Governance Checklist

Before a final funding decision, confirm:

1. The alternatives genuinely compete for the same decision attention.
2. Units and meanings are consistent within every input field.
3. Important missing values are not silently replaced by invented numbers.
4. Evidence sources are recorded and low-confidence evidence is visible.
5. The baseline weights reflect the decision context or are treated only as a starting specification.
6. Robustness results are interpreted as sensitivity to specifications, not statistical confidence probabilities.
7. CAPEX and budget are reviewed separately from Implementation Complexity.
8. Safety, legal, ethical, regulatory and procurement constraints are assessed outside the frontier model.
9. Organisational answerability is reviewed before approval.
10. The final human decision, rationale and date are recorded for audit.

A frontier result means “not clearly dominated under the declared model.” It does not mean “must be funded.”
